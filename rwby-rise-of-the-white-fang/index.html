RWBY Rise of the White Fang FAQ

What kind of game is RWBY Rise of the White Fang?▼

→It is an adults-only fan visual novel by CorrhenGames, set after Salem's defeat and centered on a renewed White Fang threat and a plot-heavy corruption story.

Is version 0.4 complete?▼

→No. The project is in development. Version 0.4 is a current public chapter rather than the finished story.

Can I play without installing the PC build?▼

→Yes. Confirm the age notice and launch the independent browser conversion in the game frame.

Why does loading vary between scenes?▼

→Artwork and WebM sequences load progressively. A scene with uncached media can take longer than one that the browser has already received.

Where are browser saves stored?▼

→Ren'Py saves are normally stored in the game host's browser storage. They can be isolated or removed by private mode, cleanup tools, a different browser, or cleared site data.

What is a safe replay method?▼

→Keep one stable save, change one conversation or route goal, and compare a complete sequence before changing another variable.

Can mobile browsers run it?▼

→Some can, but landscape orientation, free memory, and keeping the tab foregrounded are important. A current desktop browser is more dependable.

Is the game suitable for minors?▼

→No. It contains explicit adult material, corruption and mind-control themes, coercion, and sexual content. It is restricted to adults aged 18 or older.

Where are official updates published?▼

→CorrhenGames' itch.io page is the authoritative source for downloads, development news, and future versions.

Quick Notes

  • Adults 18+ only; the embedded build contains explicit mature material.
  • Version 0.4 is in development and does not represent the completed story.
  • Large scene media loads on demand, so the first visit to a sequence can pause.
  • Use several named saves and export a backup when the Ren'Py menu permits it.
  • Visit CorrhenGames' itch.io release page for official downloads and updates.
